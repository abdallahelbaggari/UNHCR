Humanitarian Hub

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Independent app. Humanitarian Hub is an independent, community-built app for the Pi Network. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the UN Refugee Agency, the United Nations or any government.

What stays on your device

Your mood check-ins, document checklist, notes, emergency contacts, language and settings are saved only in your browser's storage on your device. We do not receive them. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Pi sign-in

If you sign in with Pi, the Pi app shares your Pi username and a user ID with this app so it can show your name and your payment history. We never see or store your passphrase, seed phrase or private keys.

Payments

Payments are made inside the Pi app. Our server checks each payment with Pi Network (amount, status and transaction ID) before approving and completing it. Supporting the app is not a donation to the UN Refugee Agency.

Crisis alerts

To show current disaster alerts, our server requests public data from GDACS (Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System). No personal data is sent.

Assistant questions

If you use the assistant, the question you type is sent through our server to an AI service to produce an answer. Do not include names, ID numbers or other personal details in your questions. We do not keep your questions.

Feedback

Feedback opens your own email app with a message to us. It contains only what you choose to write.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers or selling of data.
  • No location tracking. If you tap “detect my location”, your device's position is sent once to OpenStreetMap's public service to find your country, so the app can show that country's services. It is not stored.
  • No collection of refugee status, identity documents or case information.

Children

The app is meant for adults and for families using it together.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses data.
